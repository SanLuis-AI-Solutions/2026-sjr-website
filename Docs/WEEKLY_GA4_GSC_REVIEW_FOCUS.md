# Weekly GA4/GSC Review Focus

Updated: 2026-07-02

## Business Goal

Measure whether search traffic is turning into repair bookings, not just visits.

## Primary Questions

1. Are more people reaching `/book`?
2. Are booking form starts and submissions increasing?
3. Are homepage search clicks and CTR improving?
4. Are priority local queries moving closer to top 3?
5. Did any recent site change create a production issue?

## Priority Search Clusters

Track these first:

- jewelry repair near me
- watch repair near me
- ring resizing near me
- ring sizing near me
- pearl restringing
- Pasadena jewelry repair
- Pasadena watch repair

## Booking Funnel Metrics

Review these every week:

- `/book` page views
- booking form starts
- booking submit attempts
- booking success outcomes
- booking pending outcomes
- booking errors
- phone clicks from `/book`

## Search Metrics

Review these every week:

- GSC clicks
- GSC impressions
- CTR
- average position
- top landing pages
- top queries by impressions with low CTR
- queries ranking positions 4-15

## Production Health Checks

Confirm:

- `/book` loads successfully
- custom date selector is visible and readable
- no visible "Skip to main content" button
- header/footer logo renders
- no broken images on priority pages
- GA4 production events still fire

## Decision Rule

If traffic is low, do not overreact to one-week conversion swings. Prioritize:

1. More qualified local visibility through GBP and reviews.
2. Higher CTR on pages already getting impressions.
3. Removing obvious booking friction.

## Weekly Output

The weekly monitor should end with:

- What improved
- What declined
- What is unclear because traffic is low
- One highest-leverage action for the next week
