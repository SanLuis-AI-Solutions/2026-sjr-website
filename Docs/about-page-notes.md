## Timeline Highlights (Our Journey Through the Years)
- 1985 — The Beginning: first location in a Houston flea market, launched with basic tools and a clear vision.
- 1997 — Expansion & Growth: opened an official store on Spencer Hwy in South Houston, expanded services.
- 2008 — Bigger & Better: moved to the Fairmont location with a larger store and more merchandise.
- 2020 — A Legacy Continues: next generations joined to continue the family legacy.
