# High-Value SEO, GEO, and AEO Content Standard

Use this standard before publishing or refreshing any Susie's Jewelry Repair page. The goal is to avoid commodity content and give Google, AI answer engines, and customers a specific reason to trust and retrieve the page.

## Zero-Help Gate

Do not publish a new article or service-area page if it could be written without Susie's-specific evidence. Request or collect at least one of these inputs first:

- A real repair scenario from the shop, with identifying customer details removed.
- A photo or visual observation from intake, bench inspection, repair, or pickup.
- A specific decision pattern the shop sees repeatedly, such as when a stopped watch is not just a battery.
- A local workflow detail, such as how quote-first intake changes the trip from Pasadena, Deer Park, La Porte, Webster, Friendswood, or Clear Lake.
- A concrete timing, pricing, or approval boundary the shop can stand behind.

## Required Page Elements

Every commercial article and service-area page should include:

- A direct answer in the first screen for AEO extraction.
- A "why this matters now" angle tied to loss prevention, timing, cost clarity, or sentimental risk.
- At least three internal links: one service page, one related article, and one conversion page such as `/quote` or `/book`.
- Specific anchor text that matches the customer's question, not generic "learn more" copy.
- FAQ entries that answer actual pre-booking questions in plain language.
- Last-updated or sitemap freshness metadata through the existing sitemap pipeline.

## Rewrite Standard

Before marking a page done, ask:

> Could another AI generate this exact page without Susie's local workflow, repair judgment, or customer scenario data?

If yes, the page is still too generic. Add local proof, a clearer shop point of view, and stronger decision guidance before publishing.
