## Why This Tranche Exists

The site already has deep coverage for:

- ring sizing cost and timing

Adding more posts in those lanes would mostly rephrase existing search intent instead of expanding organic coverage.

This tranche is designed to support the broader homepage opportunity cluster while opening new commercial-intent territory for the blog.

## First Post Brief

### Topic

`How much does jewelry repair cost in Pasadena?`

### Target intent

- commercial
- pricing
- local decision-stage search

### Existing posts it is not duplicating

- `cost-to-resize-gold-ring-pasadena`
- `how-much-does-pearl-restringing-cost-pasadena`
- `ring-sizing-guide`
- `watch-battery-replacement`

This post is broader than any current pricing article and acts as a quote-first guide across multiple repair categories.

### Required angles

- clear starting-price framing without pretending every repair has one flat number
- explain why quotes vary by metal, condition, stone security, custom work, clasp condition, or structural rebuild
- reinforce `100% in-house` handling and `no work without approval`
- emphasize Pasadena / Fairmont Pkwy / nearby-city convenience

### Internal links

- `/quote`
- `/services/watch-repair`
- `/services/ring-sizing`
- `/services/stone-setting`
- `/services/necklace-repair`
- `/services/heirloom-restoration`

### CTA standard

- primary: `Get Fast Quote`
- secondary: `Book Repair`
- trust framing: `your jewelry stays on-site`, `same day / next day for many standard repairs`, `clear approvals before work starts`

## Duplicate Check Standard

No new post moves forward unless it passes all three checks:

1. It does not duplicate an existing title or slug intent.
2. It does not duplicate an existing commercial intent paired with the same core service.
3. It adds a net-new decision, comparison, or pricing question that the current library does not already answer.

## Success Metrics

- organic impressions and clicks to `/blog/*`
- assisted quote or booking starts from article CTAs
- stronger internal-link support for homepage and priority service pages
- no increase in obvious content cannibalization between overlapping post clusters
