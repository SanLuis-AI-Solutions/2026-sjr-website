# NotebookLM Blog Workflow

- Updated: 2026-04-12
- Scope: Phase 3B net-new blog work only

## Rule

Reuse the current SJR notebooks. Do not create a new notebook unless a real source gap appears after the first two net-new posts.

## Notebook Order

1. `SJR Services`
- use for repair categories, cost drivers, safe timing claims, and internal-link targets

2. `SJR Local SEO And Coverage`
- use for Pasadena framing, nearby-city mentions, and near-me/local concerns

3. `SJR Competitors`
- use for comparison gaps, chain-store weaknesses, and mail-in / outsourced-repair contrasts

4. `SJR Reviews And Voice`
- use for objections, trust language, speed proof, transparency phrasing, and reassurance copy

## Required Research Output For Each New Post

Each research pass must produce:

- primary target intent
- why it is net-new
- existing posts it is not duplicating
- 4 to 6 section outline
- 3 FAQ candidates
- internal-link map
- trust language to repeat
- local modifiers and nearby cities to mention if relevant

## Prompt Pattern

For each notebook, ask only for the part it owns:

- `SJR Services`
  - what categories, cost drivers, and timing promises are safe to mention?
- `SJR Local SEO And Coverage`
  - what Pasadena / near-me framing and nearby-city cues should support this post?
- `SJR Competitors`
  - what chain, kiosk, or mail-in weakness can this post exploit?
- `SJR Reviews And Voice`
  - what customer fear, objection, or reassurance language should shape the CTA and trust framing?

## Writing Guardrails

- do not invent exact prices unless they already exist in approved internal source material
- keep pricing language directional when the final quote depends on inspection
- avoid generic jewelry-history filler
- stay commercial or decision oriented
- favor local, in-house, quote-first differentiation over generic SEO wording

## Approval Checklist

Before a post is added to `BLOG_POSTS`, confirm:

- it passed the duplicate check
- its CTA path is clear
- it links to at least three relevant money pages
- its FAQ answers do not repeat another article's exact core promise
- it supports either homepage demand or a priority service page without cannibalizing an existing post
