# Claude Full-Site Audit Task

You are conducting a strategic full-site audit for:

- `https://www.susiesjewelryrepair.com`

Your goal is not to admire the site. Your goal is to identify what will most increase:

- organic traffic
- AI-sourced visibility
- qualified repair bookings
- quote starts
- calls and storefront visits

## Your Job

Focus on:

- strategic SEO positioning
- GEO and AEO readiness
- local-intent trust and entity clarity
- conversion architecture
- content gaps that affect revenue, not vanity
- whether the site is likely to be recommended by AI systems for local jewelry repair intent

Do not spend your time on minor cosmetic notes unless they affect trust, clarity, or conversion.
