# Stuller Showcase Integration Brief

## Objective
Integrate the Stuller Showcase (Jeweler Showcase) into the SJR website as a premium, performance-optimized "Virtual Showroom".

## Key Requirements
- **Performance**: Must not degrade the site's Lighthouse scores (especially on mobile).
- **Branding**: Full "White-Label" experience. No Stuller-branded headers or footers.
- **UX**: Professional intro state that builds trust and anticipation before loading the catalog.
- **Responsive**: Seamless infinite scroll (resizing) without double scrollbars.

## Stakeholders
- Susie (Master Jeweler)
- Customers seeking custom jewelry inspiration
