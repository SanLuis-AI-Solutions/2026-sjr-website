# SEO/GEO/AEO Homepage Copy Optimization Options

## 1. Objective
Create high-intent, premium copy for the Susie’s Jewelry Repair (SJR) homepage that maximizes local visibility (GEO), answers key user trust questions (AEO), and ranks for core service terms (SEO) while maintaining a luxury tone.

## 2. Inputs Reviewed
- `src/components/hero.tsx`: Current H1 and Subhead structure.
- `src/app/layout.tsx`: Current site-wide metadata (Title/Description).
- `GEMINI_TASK.md`: Specific entity and keyword constraints.

---

## 3. Copy Options

### Option A: The Local Authority (GEO Focus)
*Best for: Dominating "Near Me" searches and local Pasadena intent.*

- **Headline (H1):** `Pasadena’s Premier In-House Jewelry Repair.`
- **Subhead:** `On-site master craftsmanship for your most cherished pieces. Transparent pricing, expert care, and Pasadena’s most trusted workshop.`
- **Meta Description:** `Discover Pasadena’s most trusted in-house jewelry repair. Master craftsmanship on-site with transparent pricing and fast turnaround for rings, watches, and more.`
- **GEO/AEO Rationale:** Anchors the location "Pasadena" at the front of the H1. Explicitly labels the business as a "workshop" and "on-site" to answer the common AEO query: "Where is the jewelry actually repaired?"

### Option B: The Expertise Specialist (AEO/SEO Focus)
*Best for: Answering specific service questions and ranking for "Master" intent.*

- **Headline (H1):** `Master Jewelry Repair in Pasadena, Done On-Site.`
- **Subhead:** `Precision repairs by expert craftsmen. From ring sizing to watch restoration, your jewelry never leaves our secure Pasadena workshop.`
- **Meta Description:** `Expert jewelry repair in Pasadena. Our on-site master craftsmen handle everything from ring sizing to watch repair with precision and care. Get a fast quote today.`
- **GEO/AEO Rationale:** Focuses on "Master Jewelry Repair" as a high-authority keyword. Uses a "never leaves" value prop to address AEO safety concerns and reinforces the "In-House" promise.

### Option C: The Timeless Luxury (Premium Focus)
*Best for: Brand positioning and attracting high-ticket restoration clients.*

- **Headline (H1):** `Exquisite Jewelry Repair & Master Craftsmanship in Pasadena.`
- **Subhead:** `Preserving your legacy with in-house expertise. Experience premium jewelry restoration and repair where quality meets transparency.`
- **Meta Description:** `Elevate your jewelry repair experience in Pasadena. Our in-house master craftsmen provide exquisite restoration and repair services with the care your legacy deserves.`
- **GEO/AEO Rationale:** Uses "Exquisite" and "Legacy" to elevate the tone. Maintains "In-House" and "Pasadena" to ensure local relevance while positioning the service as a higher-tier alternative to mall jewelers.

---

## 4. Recommended Option Set: Option A (The Local Authority)
**Why:** It strikes the perfect balance between premium tone and aggressive local SEO. Putting "Pasadena" first in the H1 is the strongest signal for GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) when users ask Siri/Google "Where is a jewelry repair shop in Pasadena?" It also uses "workshop" in the subhead, which is a high-trust word that implies on-site expertise better than "store" or "shop."

## 6. Risks/Notes
- **Character Limits:** Meta descriptions are optimized to ~155 characters to avoid truncation in SERPs.
- **H1 Length:** Option A is concise and fits the current "cinematic" layout of the hero section without breaking into too many lines on mobile.
- **Entity Density:** All options include "Pasadena", "Jewelry Repair", and "In-House/On-Site" to satisfy the core ranking requirements.
