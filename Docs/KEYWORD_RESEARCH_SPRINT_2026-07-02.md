## Competitor Notes

The competitive pattern is clear:

- Big brands and chains cover broad repair categories: ring resizing, prong repair, watch batteries, crystal replacement, band adjustment, chain/clasp repair, rhodium, pearl restringing.
- Local competitors and watch repair pages often lead with convenience claims: same-day battery swaps, while-you-wait, water-resistance checks, warranty, and appointment booking.
- Searchers care about price, timing, whether work is in-house, and whether their item is safe.

Susie's advantage should be positioned as: local Pasadena repair, in-house assessment, clear approval before work, same-day/next-day where applicable, 90-day workmanship warranty, and booking-first intake.
