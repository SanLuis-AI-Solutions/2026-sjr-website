Frequently Asked Questions

How much do estimates cost?

expand_more
At Susie's, we offer complimentary verbal estimates for all jewelry repairs. Written insurance appraisals are available for a flat fee of $125 per item.

What is the typical turnaround time?

expand_more
Most standard repairs like ring sizing or stone tightening are completed within 5-7 business days. More complex restoration work or special-order parts may take 2-3 weeks. We always provide a specific timeline with your estimate.

Do you replace watch batteries?

expand_more
Yes, we provide while-you-wait battery replacement for most luxury brands. Pressure testing for waterproof watches is also available and typically takes 24-48 hours.

Can you resize my engagement ring?

expand_more
Absolutely. We specialize in resizing complex settings including platinum, gold, and eternity bands. Most resizing is completed in our on-site Houston workshop.

How do you handle stone replacement?

expand_more
We source ethically mined and lab-grown stones to match your original's color, cut, and clarity. Each stone is set by our master goldsmith under a high-powered microscope.

Is there a warranty on repairs?

expand_more
Yes, we offer a 1-year craftsmanship warranty on all repair services. This covers the specific work performed and materials provided.

Is my jewelry insured while with you?

expand_more
Your pieces are fully insured under our jeweler's block policy from the moment they are checked in until they are returned to you.
