# Keyword Research

## Workflow
1. Define target personas and intent buckets
2. Build seed list and expand
3. Cluster by topic and intent
4. Prioritize by impact and difficulty
5. Map to pages and content briefs

## Outputs
- Keyword clusters
- Priority list
- Intent map
- Content brief outlines
